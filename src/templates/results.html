ADVANCED STATS

Z
Standard deviations above (+) or below (−) the average of the top 500 players in that category. Every category counts equally, and the total is the average across categories.
SGP
Standings points the player is worth in that category, based on how much of the stat it has taken to gain a point in past seasons. Rate stats are scaled by playing time.
#
Rank among all players in the pool, 1 being best.
VR
Versus replacement: the Z or SGP minus the average of the 5 best free agents at the player's position for that stat (his lowest-replacement eligible position if he has several).

ADVANCED STATS

Z
Standard deviations above (+) or below (−) the average of the top 250 players of the same type (batters or pitchers) in that category. Every category counts equally, and the total is the average across categories.
SGP
Standings points the player is worth in that category, based on how much of the stat it has taken to gain a point in past seasons. Rate stats are scaled by playing time.
#
Rank among all players in the pool, 1 being best.
VR
Versus replacement: the Z or SGP minus that of a replacement player: the average of the 5 best free agents at the player's position, ranked by total (the same players for every stat, so the stats add up to the total). If he has several positions, the one with the lowest replacement is used.
